I contributed to the **Idris Frex** project, a library which offers a new approach to **synthesising algebraic proofs** in dependently-typed programming languages, based on **free extensions** from **universal algebra**.

My **contribution** was to extend the library with the construction of the free algebra for the distributive combination of theories, as well as with some additional solvers.

Frex offers a new approach to synthesising algebraic proofs in dependently-typed programming languages, based on free extensions from universal algebra. Frex provides a common interface to algebraic reasoning, supporting the construction of terms from variables and operations, then automatically extending built-in propositional equality to support user-defined equations.
