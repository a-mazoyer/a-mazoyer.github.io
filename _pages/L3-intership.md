# L3 internship

I completed a 6 weeks reasearch internship supervised by [Martin Bodin](https://mbodin.github.io/) at [Centre Inria de l'Université Grenoble Alpes](https://www.inria.fr/en/inria-center-universite-grenoble-alpes) (June - July 2025).

The topic was _"Function analysis in Rocq: bridging informal reasoning and formal proofs"_. With the help of proof analysis and formal methods, I was able to create a datastructure to bridge the gap between pen & paper proofs and formals proofs in Rocq. This is part of a bigger project whose goal is to help first-year college students acquire the necessary mathematical rigor to continue their curriculum.
