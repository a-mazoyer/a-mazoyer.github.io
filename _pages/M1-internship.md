# M1 internship

I completed a 12 weeks reasearch internship supervised by [Ohad Kammar](https://denotational.co.uk/) in the [Laboratory for Foundations of Computer Science](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/lfcs/research/programming-languages-and-foundations) at the [University of Edinburgh School of Informatics](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/) (May - July 2026).

The topic was _"Varieties of Semiring Solvers via Multicategorical and Algebraic Constructions"_. With the help of category theory, we were able to modularly combine free algebras for component theories to get a free algebra for distributive combinations of theories. Combined with existing work, the let us implement a variety of semiring solvers in Idris 2.
